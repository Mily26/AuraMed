## Brand & Style

The design system is centered on mindful adherence and quiet health rituals. Traditional clinical reminders induce anxiety through harsh alarms, sterile palettes, and urgent neon alerts. In contrast, this design system treats daily medication and supplement routines as restorative, sensory pauses—transforming regimen into mindful practice.

The aesthetic fuses warm residential minimalism with tactile editorial refinement. The emotional response is centered, capable, and unhurried. Layouts breathe with generous, comforting whitespace and soft architectural forms. Visual elements draw heavily from apothecary porcelain, pressed linen, and natural earthenware, replacing mechanical friction with smooth organic interactions and soft, grounded transitions.

## Colors

The palette rejects synthetic medical blue and harsh alarm reds in favor of earthen, biological hues that harmonize with daily life:

- **Canvas & Underlays:** Built on alabaster and warm linen tones (`#FBF9F5` base canvas, `#F5F1EB` subtle underlay). These eliminate glare and soften optical fatigue.
- **Surfaces & Tiers:** Soft sand and pressed clay tones (`#EFEAE1` and `#E6DFD5`) form floating pill containers and card backgrounds, offering tactile presence without artificial shadow cast.
- **Primary (Wellness & Completion):** Muted Sage (`#7C9082`). Serves as the celebratory anchor for confirmed doses, streak continuities, and positive adherence metrics.
- **Secondary (Attentive Alert & Caution):** Warm Terracotta (`#D08C70` / `#C26D53`). Signals missed dosages, refills, and timely warnings with organic urgency rather than clinical alarm.
- **Neutral (Content & Typography):** Deep Espresso (`#24211E`) for high-contrast primary typography and Deep Driftwood (`#5C5650`) for metadata, timestamps, and secondary annotations.

## Typography

The type system blends classical literary warmth with functional clarity. 

- **Display & Headings (EB Garamond):** Brings an authoritative, serene editorial aesthetic reminiscent of historical pharmacopeias and natural wellness literature. It establishes a restful cadence for screen transitions, time segments (e.g., *Morning Ritual*), and medication titles.
- **Interface, Numbers & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides geometric crispness and high legibility across small phone viewports. It handles dosages (e.g., *100 mg*), schedule times, telemetry, and action labels cleanly without decorative distraction.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a fluid, single-column mobile-first flow that expands into structured cards on larger viewports. A 4-column structure drives mobile screens, expanding to 8 columns on tablets. 

Layouts emphasize spacious horizontal padding and uncrowded vertical margins. Visual elements avoid edge collisions to protect a calm sensory state. Containers float within the safe viewport area, surrounded by a continuous alabaster canvas. Spacing between dosage clusters utilizes `space-xl` to distinctly divide chronobiological windows (Morning, Midday, Evening, Bedtime) without reliance on heavy dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids drop shadows and stark directional lighting. Depth is achieved via organic tonal layering and diffused earthen undertones:

- **Base Layer:** The canvas sits at `#FBF9F5`.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Pill Trackers):** Solid clay surface `#EFEAE1` defined with an organic low-contrast outline of `1px solid rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Trays & Popovers):** Warm alabaster `#FFFFFF` elevated with an ultra-diffused, ambient ground shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(92, 86, 80, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Depressed State:** Micro-recessed tactile feedback created using subtle inset color shifts to `#E6DFD5` without hard inner shadows.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is driven by soft pill and organic pebble contours (`roundedness: 3`). Corners echo smoothed river stones and apothecary caplets. 

- **Cards & Dosage Modules:** Sculpted with deep radii (2rem / 32px), creating a friendly, protective presence.
- **Actions, Inputs, and Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`), ensuring thumbs meet soft silhouettes across all primary touchpoints.
- **Progress Indicators:** Capsule-shaped tracks and indicator bars that reinforce the physical form of wellness tablets.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Confirm/Take Dose):** Full pill silhouette, filled with Muted Sage (`#7C9082`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), with a gentle scale-down animation (0.97) on touch.
- **Secondary Action (Snooze/Skip):** Sand-tinted surface (`#EFEAE1`), Charcoal text (`#24211E`), no border.
- **Floating Confirmation Bar:** Sticky bottom pill dock housing quick-log gestures, floating 16px above the device home indicator.

### Dose & Medication Cards
- **Dose Unit Card:** Large pill-rounded sand containers (`#EFEAE1`) featuring medication name in *EB Garamond*, paired with dosage and form in *Plus Jakarta Sans*. Cards integrate an illustrative silhouette icon of the capsule, liquid vial, or tablet.
- **Completed State:** Transitions smoothly to a tinted sage surface (`rgba(124, 144, 130, 0.15)`) with an integrated sage checkmark.

### Progress & Timeline Indicators
- **Chronobiological Time Wheel / Ribbon:** Horizontal scrolling sequence of rounded pill days. The active day is filled with Espresso (`#24211E`) and crisp white type; upcoming days use Sand (`#EFEAE1`). A subtle sage dot underneath indicates 100% adherence.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Dose Counters & Time Selectors:** Fully rounded input fields filled with `#F5F1EB`, utilizing large, legible numerals in Plus Jakarta Sans. Outlines remain low-contrast (`rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.08)`), transitioning to Sage on focus.
- **Toggles & Checkmarks:** Custom organic pebble switches. The toggle knob is a smooth porcelain white circle nested within a muted sand-to-sage sliding track.

### Alert Banners & Refill Chips
- **Refill Notice:** Gentle terracotta tinted capsule (`rgba(208, 140, 112, 0.12)`) featuring a terracotta dot indicator and Espresso label, providing a calm reminder instead of an alarming interruption.
